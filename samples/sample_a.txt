Plagiarism detection is the process of locating instances of plagiarism within a
document or a body of work. Automated similarity detection systems compare the text
of submitted documents against one another to find passages that are suspiciously
similar. A typical pipeline first extracts the raw text, then normalizes it by
lowercasing and removing punctuation, and finally compares the documents using a
similarity algorithm such as cosine similarity or the Jaccard index. When the
computed score exceeds a configured threshold, the pair of documents is flagged for
manual review by an instructor.
