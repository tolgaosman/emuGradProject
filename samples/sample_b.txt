Plagiarism detection is the task of finding cases of plagiarism inside a document or
a collection of work. Automated similarity detection tools compare the text of the
submitted documents with one another to discover passages that are suspiciously
alike. A common pipeline first extracts the raw text, then normalizes it by
lowercasing and stripping punctuation, and finally compares the documents with a
similarity algorithm such as cosine similarity or the Jaccard index. When the
resulting score passes a configured threshold, the pair of documents is flagged for
manual review by a teacher.
